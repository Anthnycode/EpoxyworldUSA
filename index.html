85+ gloss

Gloss

Wet, mirror-like shine that reflects lights and pulls the most depth out of metallic pigments. Our default for metallic floors.

  • Deepest color and 3D movement
  • Brightens a room by bouncing light
  • Shows dust, tire marks and fine scratches sooner
  • Can be slick when wet, so we add grip media on request
Under 15 gloss

Matte

Flat and modern, close to the look of sealed natural stone or polished concrete. No reflections at all.

  • Quiet, architectural look for homes and studios
  • Best at hiding scratches and wear
  • No glare from windows or lighting
  • Mutes metallic shimmer, so it suits flake and solid colors best

How we install

Five steps,
done right

Coatings fail when prep gets skipped. We grind, test and repair before a single drop of resin goes down.

  1. Grind

    Diamond grinders open the concrete to a profile the epoxy can bite into. Dust is captured by vacuum.

  2. Repair

    Cracks, spalls and pitting get filled and ground flush. We test for moisture before coating.

  3. Base coat

    A 100% solids epoxy primer soaks in and bonds to the slab.

  4. Broadcast or pour

    Flake is broadcast to refusal, or metallic is poured and worked into its pattern.

  5. Topcoat

    A clear topcoat in gloss or matte seals it with UV and chemical resistance. Flake floors get a polyaspartic topcoat.

Questions

Good to
know

How long does an install take?

Installs take a minimum of 3 days, from grinding and repairs through the final topcoat. Larger floors, heavy repairs or detailed designs can take longer.

Will hot tires peel it up?

Hot-tire pickup happens when a thin coating sits on poorly prepared concrete. We diamond grind every slab and use a 100% solids base coat and a tough clear topcoat (polyaspartic on flake floors), which holds up to hot tires.

Is it slippery when wet?

Any gloss floor gets slick with water on it. We can add an anti-slip aggregate to the topcoat at no extra charge, and flake floors already have texture.

What does a garage floor cost?

Cost depends on the system you choose and the square footage of your floor. The instant quote gives you a price range in seconds, and we confirm the final price after a free on-site estimate.

Can you coat over cracks and stains?

Yes. Cracks are filled and ground flush, and grinding removes most surface stains. Deep oil contamination gets degreased before we coat.
